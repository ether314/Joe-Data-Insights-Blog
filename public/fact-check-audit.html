Method. Each post was audited in its own commit cycle: load-bearing claims extracted and checked against the cited primary sources (OECD, USDA/AMS/NASS/ERS, CMS, KFF/Mercer/Evernorth, Alphaliner/Sea-Intelligence/UNCTAD, STB/AAR/BTS, FMCSA/DAT, BLS/FAA-OPSNET, USGS MCS, worldsteel/IEA, USBR/CRS, GAO/JBREC, RAND/AHA/HCCI, UN Comtrade/UNEP, BIS/CPMI/FSB). Corrections were applied as minimal diffs with inline [Source Tag] anchors and structured Sources footers. Desk estimates were not altered — only verified as internally consistent and clearly labeled. A later voice-persona pass changed prose only; numeric-token frequency diffing against the pre-style baseline confirmed zero changes to any statistic, table row, or footnote URL.
